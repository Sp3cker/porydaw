Fixture, sequence and row constraints
crossTpbNotePaste copies at 24 TPQN and pastes into a 48-division rig at edit
cursor 24. mergeTimeRangeAndUndo pins the 48-to-24 rescale rule on a probe clip
before writing a 48-TPB clip to the system clipboard and pasting at cursor 24.
emptyLaneMergeIsNoop pastes an empty volume-lane clip at cursor 120 and
requires no undo entry. tiledTimePasteUndoesOneTileAtATime pastes a span-96
tile twice and undoes one tile at a time. No _data rows and no loops expand
sites in this file; every site executes once per method run.
