Status and evidence
The native voicegroupsave suite is retired: it is not in
src/checks/CMakeLists.txt or checkcatalog, so its assertions are a
historical oracle, not runnable coverage. This ledger maps the
VoicegroupBrowser LIST contract (stable rows, loading overlay, selector,
selection/reveal, used marks, voiceChanged refresh, audition and edit
intents) to runnable Swift checks over VoiceListController. MATCHED means a
corresponding Swift predicate exists and runs through the named entry
point; PARTIAL means the Swift check covers the predicate with a stated
difference; NATIVE identifies retained native-window/Qt obligations a
presenter-level check cannot establish; GAP means no equivalent assertion.
None of these labels authorizes deletion or claims whole-widget
equivalence: the editor panel, sample picker popup, type icons, and dock
chrome are out of scope and stay NATIVE.

Execution and ownership
runVoiceListChecks (src/checks/voicelist/VoiceListChecks.swift and
VoiceListIntentChecks.swift) exercises the presenter over synthetic
BankSlotView fixtures — no project needed.
runVoiceListSessionChecks (src/checks/voicelist/voicelist_session.swift)
stages the shared swiftcore project, opens mus_session_test through
ProjectService/DocumentSession, and drives refresh(from:),
refreshUsedVoices(from:), and applyVoiceEdit against the real bank
pipeline. Both are @MainActor internal functions registered in
SessionChecks.swift's swiftcore suite.

QML bridge status
VoiceListController is @QtBridgeable: rows is a QListModel of 128 stable
VoiceListRowHandle objects whose tracked fields rewrite in place,
argChoices is a QListModel<VoiceListArgChoice>, selector/loading/
selection state is @QtTracked, and the user actions (selectSlot,
revealSlot, commitVoicegroupSelection, pressVoice/releaseVoice,
requestSampleAudition, stopSampleAudition, requestNewVoicegroup,
requestNewSample, requestEditSample, slotIsMarkedUsed) are QML-invocable.
Owner-only seams (bindBank, setUsedVoices, setVoicegroupChoices,
setCurrentVoicegroupArg, voiceChanged, refresh, bindSession,
applyVoiceEdit, voiceDraft, requestVoiceEdit, the on* callbacks, and the
injected catalogs) are @QtIgnored. The QML panel shell itself is deferred
to the dock-lists plan's presenter/QML task; this ledger proves the model
surface only and makes no visual-QML claim.
