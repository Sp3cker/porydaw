Execution and ownership
No Swift entry point executes these checks. The original slots run under
PianoRollTest (checks/rollcheck/tst_pianoroll.h): scaleFoldOccupancy,
scaleFoldTrackScope, scaleFoldUndoLifecycle, scaleFoldRootInvariant. Each slot
builds on the per-slot PianoRollFixture (checks/rollcheck/rollcheck.h,
m_fixture) with direct document edits (doc.addNote, undo/redo) and
view.selectTrack for the scope switch; per-slot firstFreeOffScale lambdas pick
probe pitches.

Fixture, sequence and row constraints
Occupancy scans loop over all 128 pitches. C1 proves timeline-wide occupancy
with a distant-tick add; C2 adds notes on the non-selected track and switches
the selected track to prove the row set is replaced; C4 walks add, delete/undo,
and redo; C5 reclassifies the root (0 to 11) while holding occupied-pitch
geometry, revision, and camera scrollY. Each slot resets Highlight/Fold/root/scale
and closes with the undo-index plus smf restoration guards.
