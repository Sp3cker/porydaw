Fixture, sequence and row constraints

move sequences. Audition on/off key tallies expand one site per key event.
Distance cases contrast subthreshold drags (no note) with Space::One drags (one
snap cell). Velocity sequences pin the hover mark, land at 78 from a 93 seed,

and follows with a Right nudge. Undo-count holds and smf restoration guards
