Each numbered entry records one original assertion/failure site, not one test
method or one runtime execution. Loops/data rows reuse a site; their instances
are described below. QFAIL/qFail guards and helper calls are part of the contract.
MATCHED means a corresponding Swift predicate was identified and executed;
PARTIAL means a related check exists but differs in a stated way;
GAP means an equivalent Swift assertion is not established;
NATIVE-SETUP identifies a C++ harness/loader/device precondition still exercised
only by the retained native path. None of these labels authorizes deletion or
claims whole-case equivalence. Native passes do not prove the Swift replacement.

Original setup, branch guards and helper implementations at the reference
revision remain authoritative; this inventory does not replace those sources.
