Fixture, sequence and row constraints
All four cases enumerate staged EditableTrack rows with a fresh SongDocument per row at distantBase/step arithmetic. timeRangeRemove inserts three notes plus two lane-7 points and removes [base+51step, base+54step) on one track, then undoes/redoes. songWholeSongRemove stages a 3/2 signature, a 150-BPM tempo, and one note, then removes [base+61step, base+65step) whole-song and checks signature/tempo/note rescue, loop-marker stability, every chunk end closing exactly 4 steps, and undo/redo byte identity. voiceLanePoint runs the DOC_CC_VOICE add/move/delete/find lifecycle. automationLanePoints runs the CC7 plus pitch-bend plus tempo lifecycle with full undo-to-original and redo-to-edited byte and tempo-vector identity.

Execution and ownership
runTimeEditsSuite (removalAndSeams) and runEventEditsSuite (laneEditing, rawTempoAndSignatureEditing, trackEditing), dispatched through pdcSuiteRun. Synthetic fixtures are built by timeDocument/MidiFile literals plus writeLane/editTempo calls; no shared editcheck fixture file carries expects for these suites.
