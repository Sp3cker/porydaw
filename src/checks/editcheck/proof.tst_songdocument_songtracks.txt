Fixture, sequence and row constraints
Corpus cases enumerate AddTrack, DuplicateTrack, ReorderableTrack, EditableTrack, or Playable rows with a fresh SongDocument per row at distantBase/step arithmetic. trackCreateDelete adds voice-7 track, checks the tick-0 voice seed, adds a note, deletes the track. trackDuplicate duplicates the first editable track and compares full note vectors. trackMove stages tempo plus a 5/2 signature, records loop markers, and moves track 0 to last and back with undo/redo. trackMarkerName is synthetic (markerNameFile): rename, two raw inserts ("[", "]["), and a move must revert together. trackDeleteRescue deletes the editable track and checks loop stability across undo. trackRename checks raw bare-name counts, timeline names, trim/reject/clear semantics, and undo/redo. songTimeSignature runs 3/3 then 7/2 set, move, delete. loopCfgUndoRedo edits loop ticks and masterVolume, then cycles full undo/redo comparing bytes, config, and the redone divergence.
