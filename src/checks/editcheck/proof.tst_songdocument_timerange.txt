Fixture, sequence and row constraints
All nine cases are synthetic over timeRangeFile() (format 1, division 24, empty plus C0/C1 program tracks, end tick 200). timeRangeRemoveRippleTrim loops earlierEnd over {100, 80, 70} for the trim-vs-ripple boundary, then loops duplicate false/true for byte-identical duplicate/insert undo/redo. timeRangeNoOps covers empty/invalid/sentinel-kNoTick rejections. timeRangeInsertScopeAndSplit covers note split with identity retention, lane-scoped insertion (selected vs untouched lanes and end ticks), and track-scoped insertion (shifted vs untouched tracks and end ticks). timeRangeUnterminated splits an unterminated note and scans raw note bytes. timeRangeDuplicateClippingAndOrder covers contained/crossing duplication with fresh identities plus raw end-before-on ordering at 720/820. timeRangeSignatureAndOrphans covers whole-song signature duplication and orphan byte ordering. timeRangeAutomationSeamsAndDefaults covers lane-seam duplication, a nine-lane default-value table loop, tempo duplication with timeline observation, voice duplication, and downstream duplication. timeRangeWholeSong duplicates meta/signature/tempo/note whole-song. timeRangeInsertBlankOverflow covers max-tick rejection.
