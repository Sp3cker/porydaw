Fixture, sequence and row constraints
Collision-reject case is synthetic: five scenarios crossed with both selection orders (reverse false/true) over a conductor plus single-program fixture (end tick 200). Scenarios cover plain moves, leftward moves, same-pitch batch collisions, resize-left into collision, and a second scenario-4 resize variant; each asserts full SavedDocState mismatch emptiness, then redoes into pairing consistency and undoes back to identical bytes. The second synthetic block exercises merge-grouped moves (two grouped moves share one undo entry), an inverse move pair that must not consume an entry, identity lookup by noteId, and a tick-1 clamp.
Corpus cases (noteMoveOverlap/Merge/Batch/Collision/Rejects) enumerate every staged EditableTrack row. Each row loads a fresh SongDocument, selects the first editable track exactly as native addSongRows, and works at distantBase = max chunk end + 100*ticksPerClock with step = ticksPerClock. Literal offsets: overlap 88/90/92/94 steps, merge 100/102 steps, batch firstTick = base+110 steps with a +20-step partner, collision 138/140/142 steps, rejects firstTick = base+110 steps. Batch pitch destinations {118,114}, nudge destinations {119,115}/{120,116}, out-of-range pitch 130, and merge-flag grouping are literal.
