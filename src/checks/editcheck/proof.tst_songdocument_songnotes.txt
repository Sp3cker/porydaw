Fixture, sequence and row constraints
Resize-stop case is synthetic: a reverse false/true loop over resizeNotesDurations scenarios (capped extension, exact shrink), zero-capacity and kMaxTick rejections, per-note duration/identity/velocity preservation, undo-counted extension/shortening with byte identity, save-boundary cleanliness, and a compatible-inverse no-op asserted through full SavedDocState mismatch emptiness.
Batch-collision case is synthetic: three conflicting insertion batches looped against one SavedDocState (each must leave zero mismatch while preserving the staged redo branch), followed by exact-duplicate insertion asserting distinct assigned identities with identical tick/duration/key/velocity, plus undo/redo byte identity.
Corpus cases (noteEditingBasic/Batch/Abutting) enumerate every staged EditableTrack row at distantBase/step arithmetic. Basic covers the full gesture chain on one note: add, move by (8 steps, +3 keys), trailing resize, leading resizes (including the duration-1 clamp and end preservation), velocity set/nudge/clamp-to-127, and delete. Batch covers two-note insertion with single-entry undo/redo. Abutting covers seam-adjacent [seam-2step,seam)/[seam,seam+2step) notes with endIndex distinctness and a raw-event pairing-boundary scan.
